FM1.

MIDI CONTROLLER

Plug in a keyboard or pad controller. Its keys play the FM-1. Its knobs and pads start on the FM-1's knobs and buttons, and DEVICE shows a drawing of it where you can change any of them.

THANK YOU, JOAKIM

FM1.Pocket is a fork of fm1-emu by Joakim Eriksson.

The whole emulated FM-1 inside (its CPU, flash, screen, keys, knobs and sound) is his work, used unchanged. This app only adds a phone-first front to it.
